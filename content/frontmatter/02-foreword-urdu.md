In the Name of Allāh, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful.
:::

There is a genuine lack of sound educational literature in Urdu for Muslim
children. This gap made it necessary to produce a concise yet comprehensive
work---one that gathers the essential teachings of Islam directly from the Noble
Quran and the authentic Sunnah in a clear and orderly manner.

The distinction of this book lies in the fact that it presents those teachings
which every Muslim must know: matters related to belief (_ʿaqīdah_), worship
(_ʿibādāt_), social conduct and transactions (_muʿāmalāt_), and moral character
(_akhlāq_). These teachings have been carefully drawn from the _āyāt_ of the
Quran and the sound Prophetic traditions, and then explained in a simple,
engaging style suited to the understanding of children. Alongside this, key
issues of creed and jurisprudence have been clarified in an accessible and
meaningful way.

What further lends strength to this work is that Quranic teachings are
consistently supported by authentic Prophetic narrations, and topics of belief
and law are not treated superficially but explained with due care and clarity.
It is hoped that this book will prove beneficial not only for children but for
all those who seek to derive guidance from it.

This modest effort was completed under the supervision of Maulānā Muḥammad Idrīs
al-Kāndhlawī, may Allāh elevate his rank. It was subsequently reviewed at Nadwat
al-ʿUlamāʾ, and thereafter examined by Mawlānā Ghāzī Rashīd Aḥmad, may Allāh
have mercy upon him.

It is our hope that this work will help draw the attention of Muslims toward the
proper religious upbringing of their children, and that Allāh will make it a
means of lasting benefit.
