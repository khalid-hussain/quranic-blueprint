BLURB 1
==================
A Timeless Guide to Living Islam

What does it mean to live Islam in its entirety? Rooted in the Qurʾān and the authentic Sunnah, The Qurʾānic Blueprint by Shaykh al-Tafsīr Muḥammad Owais al-Nagrāmī al-Nadwī is a concise yet comprehensive guide to the essential dimensions of dīn, from belief and worship to character and social conduct.

Originally written in Urdu as Taʿlīm al-Qurʾān, this enduring classic distils profound guidance into clear, practical lessons for everyday life. Accessible yet deeply rooted in the sources, it speaks to readers of every age, nurturing sound belief, refining character, and bringing faith into practice.

Small in size, immense in value, this first English translation brings a timeless work to a new generation. In an age of confusion and competing voices, The Qurʾānic Blueprint offers a clear, authentic, and spiritually enriching path to understanding Islam and living by the guidance of Allah’s Book.

BLURB 2
==================
A Practical Guide to the Essentials of Islam

How do we translate the teachings of the Qurʾān and Sunnah into a way of life?

The Qurʾānic Blueprint by Shaykh al-Tafsīr Muḥammad Owais al-Nagrāmī al-Nadwī offers a clear, structured guide to the essential teachings of Islam, bringing together the foundations of faith, acts of worship, moral character, personal conduct, and social responsibilities. Grounded in Qurʾānic āyāt and authentic aḥādīth, it explains the core principles of dīn and their practical application in everyday life.

Originally written in Urdu as Taʿlīm al-Qurʾān, this timeless classic distils essential Islamic knowledge into concise, accessible lessons without compromising depth or authenticity. From strengthening belief and fulfilling the obligations of worship to cultivating noble character and fulfilling one's duties towards others, each subject illuminates how Islam guides both the individual and society.

Concise in form, comprehensive in guidance, this first English translation brings a valuable work to a new generation of readers. Whether for students beginning their Islamic studies, families seeking a reliable reference, or anyone wishing to deepen their understanding of Islam, The Qurʾānic Blueprint offers a practical foundation for learning, practising, and living the dīn in the light of the Qurʾān and authentic Sunnah.
